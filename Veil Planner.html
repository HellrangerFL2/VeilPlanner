How it works. When you click the big cookie, the game seeds its random number generator with run seed + "/" + clicks so far and rolls once against your veil's break resistance. The click count goes up after the roll, so the first square (+1) is the roll your very next click gets, and its # matches the cookie clicks your stats show right now.

Every click advances the count, even while the veil is off, so the plan stays valid after turning the veil back on. Clicks the game ignores for being too fast don't count. Golden cookie and reindeer clicks use a separate counter and aren't shown yet.

The run seed changes when you ascend, so re-import after each ascension.
